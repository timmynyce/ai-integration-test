# Product Requirement Document

Product Name: EventFlow

## Overview
EventFlow is a web-based event management platform designed for small to mid-sized organizations to create, manage, and monetize events. It reduces operational complexity while increasing attendance and revenue through built-in discovery, ticketing, and analytics.

## Goals
- Launch events in under 10 minutes
- Increase ticket conversion rates by 20%
- Reduce manual event management work by 50%
- Support up to 10,000 attendees per event (V1)

## Approved Updates

```
## Organizer Suspension & Account Recovery

### Suspension Types & Duration
- **Temporary Suspension**: 30 days (policy violation, fraud investigation)
- **Permanent Suspension**: Severe violations (illegal activity, repeated fraud, chargeback abuse)

### Reinstatement Criteria (Temporary)
- Organizer acknowledges violation and agrees to policy compliance
- Any outstanding disputes resolved
- Automatic reinstatement after 30 days if no new violations

### Appeal Process
- Organizer may appeal suspension within 14 days of notice
- Admin review and decision within 5 business days
- Decision is final; one appeal per suspension

### Data & Payout Access During Suspension
- Organizers retain read-only access to historical event data and attendee records
- Payouts for events created before suspension are processed normally
- New event creation blocked until suspension lifted

---

## Chargeback & Dispute Liability

### Chargeback Fee Allocation
- Platform absorbs chargeback fees (typically $15–$100 per dispute) for first chargeback per organizer per calendar year
- Subsequent chargebacks in same year: organizer liable for 50% of chargeback fee
- Organizers with 3+ chargebacks in 12 months flagged for suspension review

### Dispute Response SLA
- Admin initiates chargeback response within 24 hours of notification
- Evidence gathering and submission to payment processor within 5 business days
- Organizer notified of dispute status and outcome within 2 business days of processor decision

### Organizer Liability Conditions
- Organizer liable for full chargeback fee if: event was fraudulent, organizer failed to deliver tickets, or organizer refunded attendee outside platform (circumventing audit trail)
- Liability waived if chargeback is determined to be attendee fraud (e.g., stolen card)

## Approved Updates

## Fraud Flag Definition & Payout Hold Criteria

### Fraud Flag Triggers
A fraud flag is assigned when an organizer exhibits patterns indicating elevated risk, including:
- repeated chargebacks
- abnormal refund behavior
- attendee complaints or reports
- payment processor risk signals
- failed verification checks

### System Behavior
- Fraud flags trigger review of organizer activity and payouts
- Payouts may be temporarily held during investigation
- Admin review determines whether to release funds, escalate, or suspend account

### Organizer Visibility
- Organizers are notified when a fraud flag is assigned
- Organizers can view general reason categories and respond if needed
